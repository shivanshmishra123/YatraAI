# Indian Railways (IRCTC) Travel & Booking Masterclass

## Overview of Travel Classes on Indian Railways
1. **1A (First Class AC)**: The most luxurious class. 2-berth coupes and 4-berth cabins with lockable sliding doors, reading lamps, carpeted floors, and complimentary meals on Rajdhani/Duronto expresses.
2. **2A (AC 2-Tier)**: Air-conditioned coach with 6 berths per bay (4 inside, 2 along the side corridor). Wide berths with privacy curtains, individual reading lights, and fresh laundered linen kits.
3. **3A (AC 3-Tier)**: The staple choice of Indian middle-class travel. 8 berths per bay (6 inside: upper, middle, lower; 2 on the side). Air-conditioned, linen included, exceptional value.
4. **3E (AC 3-Tier Economy)**: Newly introduced modern coaches with 83 berths. Slightly tighter berth spacing than 3A but lower ticket fares and individual AC vents.
5. **CC (AC Chair Car)**: 3x2 seating arrangement in air-conditioned coaches for daytime express trains like Shatabdi and Intercity.
6. **EC & EA (Executive Chair Car / Anubhuti)**: 2x2 spacious plush seating on Vande Bharat and Shatabdi expresses with footrests and onboard catering.
7. **SL (Sleeper Class)**: Non-air-conditioned open-window sleeper coaches with 8 berths per compartment. Ceiling fans, authentic vendor tea/snacks, budget friendly.
8. **2S (Second Seating)**: Non-AC reserved bench seating for short daytime journeys.

## Booking Rules & Timelines
- **General Advance Reservation Period (ARP)**: Bookings open 60 to 120 days in advance (check current IRCTC quota schedule) at 8:00 AM IST.
- **Tatkal Booking**: Emergency quota opening one day prior to train departure from the originating station:
  - **10:00 AM IST**: AC Classes (1A, 2A, 3A, 3E, CC, EC)
  - **11:00 AM IST**: Non-AC Classes (SL, 2S)
- **Waitlist Rules**:
  - **CNF**: Confirmed berth.
  - **RAC (Reservation Against Cancellation)**: Guaranteed boarding on the train; two RAC passengers share one lower side berth until a cancellation occurs.
  - **GNWL / WL**: Waitlisted. If fully waitlisted upon final chart preparation (4 hours before departure), e-tickets are automatically cancelled and refunded.
