# Complete Travel Guide to Hampi (The Vijayanagara Empire)

## Overview
Hampi, in central Karnataka on the banks of the Tungabhadra River, is a UNESCO World Heritage site comprising over 1,600 surviving monuments spread across a surreal landscape of giant granite boulders, banana plantations, and ancient temple complexes.

## Two Halves of Hampi
- **Sacred & Royal Centers (South Bank)**: Historic stone temple complexes, bazaars, queen's baths, and elephant stables.
- **Hippie Island (Virupapur Gaddi / Sanapur, North Bank)**: Laid-back backpacker village, coracle boat rides on Sanapur Lake, and cliff sunset viewpoints.

## Top Sights
1. **Virupaksha Temple**: Active 7th-century Dravidian temple with a 50-meter gopuram dedicated to Lord Shiva; home to temple elephant Lakshmi.
2. **Vijaya Vittala Temple & Stone Chariot**: Architectural masterpiece featuring the iconic Stone Chariot (depicted on the Indian ₹50 banknote) and musical granite pillars.
3. **Hemakuta Hill**: Gentle boulder-strewn hill dotted with pre-Vijayanagara shrines, famous for sunrise and sunset views.
4. **Elephant Stables & Lotus Mahal**: Indo-Islamic royal enclosures showcasing ornate domed chambers built for royal state elephants.
5. **Matanga Hill**: The highest vantage point in central Hampi; early morning climb offers unforgettable 360-degree views of the ruins shrouded in dawn fog.

## Practical Tips
- Nearest railhead: Hosapete Junction (HPT), 13 km away, with overnight trains from Bengaluru, Hyderabad, and Goa.
- Rent a bicycle (₹150/day) or moped to navigate the sprawling stone monument complex.
