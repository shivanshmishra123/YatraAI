# Complete Travel Guide to Darjeeling (Queen of the Hills)

## Overview
Perched in the Lesser Himalayas of West Bengal at an altitude of 2,042 meters, Darjeeling is globally celebrated for its muscatel champagne black tea, the UNESCO-listed Darjeeling Himalayan Railway (Toy Train), and jaw-dropping views of Mt. Kanchenjunga (8,586 m).

## Unmissable Experiences
1. **Tiger Hill Sunrise**: Wake up at 4:00 AM to witness dawn sunlight illuminate the snowy peaks of Mt. Kanchenjunga and Mt. Everest in golden pink hues.
2. **Darjeeling Himalayan Railway (Toy Train)**: Narrow-gauge steam engine journey chugging through Batasia Loop and Ghum (India's highest railway station at 2,258 m).
3. **Tea Garden Walks & Factory Tasting**: Tour century-old estates like Happy Valley Tea Estate, Makaibari, or Glenburn to witness tea plucking and orthodox processing.
4. **Himalayan Mountaineering Institute (HMI) & Padmaja Naidu Himalayan Zoo**: Museum honoring Tenzing Norgay and dedicated breeding center for endangered Red Pandas and Snow Leopards.
5. **The Mall & Chowrasta**: Open-air heritage pedestrian promenade lined with pine trees, heritage bookshops, and horse rides.

## Regional Flavors
- Steamed pork, chicken, and vegetarian **Momos** served with fiery red fermented chili chutney.
- **Thukpa**: Warming Tibetan noodle soup packed with broth, seasonal mountain greens, and meat.
- **Darjeeling First Flush Tea**: Light amber, floral tea served without milk at heritage cafes like Nathmulls and Keventers.
