# Complete Travel Guide to Kerala (God's Own Country)

## Overview
Kerala is celebrated worldwide for its tranquil palm-fringed backwaters, spice-scented Western Ghats hill stations, Ayurvedic wellness sanctuaries, Kathakali dance theatre, and distinctive cuisine flavored with fresh coconut and curry leaves.

## Key Circuit Destinations
1. **Alleppey (Alappuzha) & Kumarakom (The Backwaters)**:
   - Network of interconnected brackish lagoons, lakes, and canals.
   - Traditional Kettuvallam (luxury thatched-roof houseboats) offering overnight cruises with on-board private chef preparing Karimeen Pollichathu (pearl spot fish).
2. **Munnar (Western Ghats Hill Station)**:
   - 1,600 meters above sea level; endless sprawling emerald tea plantations, Eravikulam National Park (home to the endangered Nilgiri Tahr), and Anamudi Peak (South India's highest summit).
3. **Fort Kochi (Historical Port City)**:
   - Giant cantilevered Chinese Fishing Nets, Mattancherry Dutch Palace, Jewish Synagogue in Jew Town, and spice trade warehouses.
4. **Wayanad (Forests & Waterfalls)**:
   - Prehistoric Edakkal Caves with Neolithic petroglyphs, Banasura Sagar Dam (India's largest earth dam), and Chembra Peak heart-shaped lake.
5. **Varkala & Kovalam (Cliff Beaches)**:
   - Varkala features unique red laterite cliffs abutting the Arabian Sea, natural mineral water springs, and Papanasam Beach holy waters.

## Best Time to Visit
- **September to March**: Pleasant, cool weather across hill stations and dry sunny days on the coast.
- **June to August (Monsoon / Karkidakam)**: Rejuvenation season. According to traditional Ayurveda, the atmosphere is dust-free and skin pores open up, making this the premier season for Ayurvedic detoxification and panchakarma treatments.

## Connectivity & Transit
- **Airports**: Cochin International Airport (COK - the world's first fully solar-powered airport), Trivandrum (TRV), Calicut (CCJ), and Kannur (CNN).
- **Railways**: Well-connected coastal mainline. Ernakulam Junction (ERS), Alappuzha (ALLP), and Thiruvananthapuram Central (TVC).
- **Water Transit**: Kerala State Water Transport Department (SWTD) runs extremely affordable public ferries connecting Alleppey, Kottayam, and village islands.

## Authentic Experiences
- **Kathakali Performance**: Classical dance-drama featuring elaborate facial makeup, vibrant costumes, and codified eye movements narrating Hindu epics (Kathakali Centre in Fort Kochi).
- **Kalaripayattu**: One of the world's oldest martial arts, showcasing acrobatic weapons training and vital pressure point techniques.
- **Sadhya Meal**: Vegetarian feast featuring 24–28 dishes served on a fresh plantain leaf during Onam and festive gatherings, ending with golden payasam.
