# Complete Travel Guide to Udaipur (The City of Lakes)

## Overview
Often called the 'Venice of the East', Udaipur in southern Rajasthan is celebrated for its serene artificial lakes, marble palaces, royal heritage, and romantic sunsets against the rolling Aravalli mountain range.

## Iconic Lakes & Waterfronts
1. **Lake Pichola**: 4 km long freshwater lake created in 1362. Highlights include boat cruises from Rameshwar Ghat past Lake Palace (Taj Lake Palace) and Jag Mandir Island.
2. **Fateh Sagar Lake**: North of Pichola, featuring Nehru Park on an island and picturesque walking paths along the promenade (Rani Road).
3. **Doodh Talai**: Small adjacent lake with musical fountain gardens and the Karni Mata Ropeway providing aerial sunset vistas over Pichola.

## Must-Visit Monuments
1. **City Palace Complex**: Rajasthan's largest palace complex, built over 400 years by the Mewar dynasty. Marvel at the Mor Chowk (peacock courtyards), Zenana Mahal, and Crystal Gallery.
2. **Jagdish Temple**: Striking 1651 Indo-Aryan temple with towering carved shikhara and intricate elephant friezes just outside the City Palace gates.
3. **Saheliyon-ki-Bari**: 'Courtyard of the Maidens', landscaped with marble fountains, lotus pools, and sculpted stone elephants designed for royal princesses.
4. **Sajjangarh (Monsoon Palace)**: Hilltop fortress perched 944 meters above sea level in the Sajjangarh Wildlife Sanctuary, famed for 360-degree sunset panoramas.
5. **Bagore Ki Haveli**: 18th-century waterfront haveli at Gangaur Ghat holding the Dharohar Folk Dance and puppet show every evening at 7 PM.

## Connectivity
- **Air**: Maharana Pratap Airport (UDR) has regular connections to Delhi, Mumbai, and Bengaluru.
- **Train**: Udaipur City (UDZ) connects directly to Delhi (Mewar Express, Chetak Express) and Jaipur.
- **Road**: Excellent 6-lane highways connecting Ahmedabad (4.5 hrs) and Jaipur (6 hrs).
