# Complete Travel Guide to Rishikesh & Haridwar

## Overview
Perched in the Himalayan foothills of Uttarakhand where the holy Ganga descends into the plains, Rishikesh is internationally renowned as the 'Yoga Capital of the World' and the premier adventure sport hub of Northern India.

## Top Experiences
1. **White Water River Rafting**: World-class Class II to Class IV rapids on the Ganga between Marine Drive / Shivpuri and Laxman Jhula. Open from mid-September to June.
2. **Bungee Jumping & Giant Swing**: India's highest fixed-platform bungee jump (83 meters) operated by certified Jump Masters at Mohan Chatti.
3. **Triveni Ghat Evening Maha Aarti**: Spectacular spiritual ceremony where hundreds of oil lamps (diyas) float down the Ganga amid Vedic hymns.
4. **The Beatles Ashram (Chaurasi Kutia)**: Historic ashram where the Beatles studied Transcendental Meditation with Maharishi Mahesh Yogi in 1968; features evocative graffiti halls.
5. **Laxman Jhula, Ram Jhula & Janki Jhula**: Suspension bridges spanning the Ganga connecting vibrant ashrams, organic vegan cafes, and yoga studios.
6. **Neer Garh Waterfalls**: Multi-tier turquoise mountain stream reached via a 2 km hike through forest trails.

## Seasons & Practicalities
- **September to November & March to May**: Ideal temperatures (15°C–30°C) for rafting, camping along sandy riverbanks, and trekking.
- **December to February**: Crisp Himalayan winter (7°C–20°C). Great for meditation retreats and temple circuits.
- **July to August**: Heavy monsoon; river rafting is suspended by government orders.
- **Food Rules**: Rishikesh is a strictly holy vegetarian and alcohol-free municipality by law.
