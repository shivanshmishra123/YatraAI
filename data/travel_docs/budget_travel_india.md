# Comprehensive Guide to Budget Travel in India

## Introduction: The Art of Traveling Smart
India is one of the most cost-effective travel destinations in the world. With strategic planning, comfortable and culturally immersive journeys can easily be accomplished on budgets ranging from ₹800 to ₹2,500 per person per day.

## Accommodation Strategies
1. **Hostels & Pod Chains**: Networks like Zostel, goSTOPS, The Hosteller, and Moustache provide clean dorm beds (₹400–₹900/night) with high-speed Wi-Fi, air conditioning, and social common rooms.
2. **Dharamshalas & Ashrams**: At spiritual centers like Rishikesh, Varanasi, Haridwar, and Tirupati, ashrams provide dignified, peaceful lodging for ₹200–₹600/night.
3. **Homestays & Guesthouses**: Booking directly with local families in Himachal, Kerala, and Rajasthan provides authentic home-cooked meals and local guidance at 40% lower cost than hotels.

## Transport Savings
- **Indian Railways (IRCTC)**: The backbone of budget domestic travel. Sleeper Class (SL) costs ₹200–₹500 for overnight journeys (500–800 km). 3-Tier Economy (3E) and 3-Tier AC (3AC) provide air-conditioned comfort with clean bedding for ₹700–₹1,200.
- **State Road Transport Corporation (SRTC) Buses**: Government buses (e.g., HRTC in Himachal, KSRTC in Karnataka/Kerala, UPSRTC in UP) are reliable, safe, and cost half as much as private aggregators.
- **Shared Cabs & Tempos**: In hilly regions like Uttarakhand, Ladakh, and Sikkim, shared Sumos and Maxx cabs cost 80% less than hiring private taxis.

## Food & Daily Sustenance
- Eat at bustling local eateries where turnover is fast: **Thalis** (unlimited lentils, vegetables, flatbreads, and rice) cost between ₹80 and ₹150 across most states.
- Clean hydration: Refill personal water bottles using certified RO water purification dispensers available at IRCTC railway platforms for ₹5 per liter, saving both money and single-use plastic.
