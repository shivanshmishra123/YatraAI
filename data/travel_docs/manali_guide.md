# Complete Travel Guide to Manali & Kullu Valley

## Overview
Nestled at the northern end of the Kullu Valley in Himachal Pradesh at an elevation of 2,050 meters, Manali is India's favored Himalayan alpine getaway. Surrounded by towering pine and deodar forests, snow-dusted peaks, and the roaring Beas River, it serves as the adventure gateway to Spiti and Ladakh.

## Key Areas & Quarters
- **Old Manali**: Bohemian atmosphere with stone-and-wood Himachali houses, apple orchards, live acoustic music cafes, and backpacker hostels.
- **The Mall Road & New Manali**: The bustling commercial town center lined with woolen shawl emporiums, Himachali handicraft shops, and restaurants.
- **Vashisht Village**: Quiet village 3 km away across the Beas River, renowned for sulfur hot water springs and ancient wooden stone temples.

## Top Attractions
1. **Hadimba Devi Temple**: Unique four-tiered pagoda-style cedar wood temple built in 1553, set in the serene Dhungri Van Vihar forest.
2. **Atal Tunnel & Sissu (Lahaul Valley)**: The 9.02 km engineering marvel beneath Rohtang Pass connecting Manali to the arid high-altitude Lahaul valley in just 45 minutes. Sissu waterfall and snow fields are breathtaking.
3. **Rohtang Pass (3,978 m)**: Legendary mountain pass with panoramic glacier views on the Pir Panjal range. (Note: Special NGT vehicle permit required; pass remains closed in winter due to heavy snow).
4. **Solang Valley**: Adventure hub for paragliding, zorbing, quad biking in summer, and skiing/snowboarding in winter.
5. **Jogini Waterfalls**: Scenic 4 km trek from Vashisht through apple orchards and pine groves ending at cascading mountain falls.
6. **Naggar Castle**: 15th-century wood-and-stone palace overlooking the Beas valley, housing the Nicholas Roerich Art Gallery.

## Seasons & Weather
- **March to June (Summer)**: Crisp mountain air (10°C–25°C). Ideal for trekking, paragliding, river rafting in the Beas River, and camping.
- **July to August (Monsoon)**: Heavy rainfall in the lower hills can trigger landslides along the Chandigarh-Manali NH-21. Travel requires careful weather monitoring.
- **October to February (Winter)**: Sub-zero temperatures and heavy snowfall in December and January. Perfect for snow sports and winter honeymoons.

## Local Himachali Gastronomy
- **Siddu**: Steamed fermented wheat bread stuffed with spiced crushed walnuts, poppy seeds, or lentils, served drenched in pure desi ghee.
- **Himachali Dham**: Traditional celebratory feast served in brass plates featuring Madra (chickpeas in rich yogurt gravy), Khatta, and Meetha Bhaat.
- **Trout Fish**: Fresh Himalayan brown and rainbow trout pan-fried with herbs, available at cafes along Old Manali and Naggar.
