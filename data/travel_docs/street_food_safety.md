# Safe Street Food Guide for Travelers in India

## The Philosophy of Eating Clean & Savory
Indian street food is legendary for its bold spices, textures, and regional diversity. By following time-tested hygiene guidelines, you can savor authentic flavors safely.

## Golden Rules for Street Food
1. **Eat Where Crowds Gather**: High local turnover ensures ingredients are freshly cooked rather than sitting in lukewarm storage. Observe if families with children are dining there.
2. **Piping Hot is Safe**: Prioritize freshly fried or boiled dishes—hot samosas straight out of the kadhai, freshly puffed puris, tandoori breads, or boiling hot tea (chai). Heat neutralizes pathogens.
3. **Hydration & Ice**: Drink only sealed, packaged mineral water from reputable brands (Bisleri, Kinley, Aquafina, Rail Neer). Avoid street beverages containing crushed block ice unless the vendor uses filtered RO water.
4. **Pani Puri Caution**: Pani puris use cold flavored mint water. Savor them only at hygiene-rated stalls that explicitly prepare pani using packaged or RO-purified water.
5. **Probiotics & Acclimatization**: Incorporate fresh plain yogurt (curd / dahi) or lassi daily; the natural probiotics support healthy digestive flora during travel transitions.
