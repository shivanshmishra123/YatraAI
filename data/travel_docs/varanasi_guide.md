# Complete Travel Guide to Varanasi (Kashi / Benares)

## Overview
Varanasi, situated on the sacred crescent of the River Ganga in Uttar Pradesh, is one of the world's oldest continually inhabited cities. Known as Kashi ('City of Light') and Benares, it is the spiritual epicenter of Hinduism and the sacred threshold between mortality and liberation (moksha).

## The Sacred Ghats of Varanasi
Varanasi has 84 historic stone ghats stretching along the west bank of the Ganga:
1. **Dashashwamedh Ghat**: The most vibrant ghat, famed for the evening Ganga Aarti performed at dusk by priests holding towering brass multi-tiered incense lamps amid conch blowing and chants.
2. **Assi Ghat**: The southernmost prominent ghat, famous for morning Subah-e-Banaras (sunrise music, yoga, and aarti) and popular student cafes.
3. **Manikarnika & Harishchandra Ghats**: The sacred cremation grounds where funeral pyres burn continuously 24/7. Visitors must maintain solemn respect: photography is strictly prohibited.
4. **Scindia & Panchganga Ghats**: Serene heritage ghats featuring half-submerged Shiva temples and grand Maratha-built stone palatial facades.

## Key Spiritual & Cultural Sites
1. **Kashi Vishwanath Temple (Golden Temple)**: Dedicated to Lord Shiva, one of the twelve Jyotirlingas. The newly built Kashi Vishwanath Corridor provides direct, expansive access from the riverbanks to the sanctum.
2. **Sarnath (10 km northeast)**: The sacred deer park where Gautama Buddha delivered his first sermon (Dhammacakkappavattana Sutta) after attaining enlightenment. Home to Dhamek Stupa, Ashoka Pillar, and the Archaeological Museum.
3. **Banaras Hindu University (BHU)**: Founded by Pandit Madan Mohan Malaviya, housing the New Vishwanath Temple (Birla Temple) and Bharat Kala Bhavan museum.
4. **Sankat Mochan Hanuman Temple**: Founded by saint-poet Goswami Tulsidas, author of the Ramcharitmanas.

## Essential Varanasi Experiences
- **Sunrise Boat Ride**: Hire a hand-rowed wooden boat or licensed motorboat at 5:30 AM from Assi Ghat to Rajghat to observe dawn rituals, bathing pilgrims, and temple silhouettes against morning mist.
- **Exploring the Galis**: The labyrinthine narrow alleys behind the ghats, filled with hidden shrines, silk weavers, cow sanctuaries, and tiny chai stalls.

## Local Delicacies
- **Kachori Sabzi & Jalebi**: The quintessential Benares breakfast of crisp dal-stuffed kachoris served with spicy hing-infused potato curry. Renowned at Ram Bhandar (Thatheri Bazaar) and Chachi Ki Dukaan.
- **Banarasi Paan**: Betel leaf crafted with gulkand, kattha, chuna, and fragrant spices.
- **Malaiyo / Makhan Malai**: Delicate winter saffron milk froth served in earthen pots garnished with pistachios.
- **Banarasi Lassi**: Thick, creamy dahi served in kulhads topped with rabdi and malai at Blue Lassi Shop near Manikarnika.

## Etiquette & Travel Advice
- Leave footwear and leather goods in cloakrooms before entering Kashi Vishwanath Temple. Carry original government ID.
- Avoid persistent touts claiming exclusive boat deals; book boats with pre-agreed rates or through verified tourism counters.
