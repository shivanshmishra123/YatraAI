# Complete Travel Guide to Andaman & Nicobar Islands

## Overview
Located in the Bay of Bengal, the Andaman Islands boast pristine turquoise waters, powder-white coral beaches, lush tropical rainforests, and some of the world's most vibrant coral reefs for scuba diving and snorkeling.

## Main Islands & Circuit
1. **Port Blair**: The capital city; home to the historic Cellular Jail (Kala Pani) with its poignant sound and light show, and Corbyn's Cove beach.
2. **Havelock Island (Swaraj Dweep)**:
   - **Radhanagar Beach (Beach No. 7)**: Ranked among Asia's top beaches for its crystal waters and sunset panoramas.
   - **Elephant Beach**: Hub for snorkeling, sea walks, jet skiing, and coral reef exploration.
3. **Neil Island (Shaheed Dweep)**:
   - Tranquil farming island famous for Bharatpur Beach, Laxmanpur Beach sunsets, and the Natural Rock Bridge formed by limestone weathering.
4. **Baratang Island**:
   - Mangrove boat safaris through dense creek tunnels to reach ancient limestone caves and mud volcanoes.

## Travel Logistics & High-Speed Ferries
- Inter-island transit between Port Blair, Havelock, and Neil is operated by private catamarans (Makruzz, Green Ocean, Nautika) taking 90–120 minutes. Advance booking is strongly advised during peak season.
- Indian citizens do not require a passport or special permit to visit standard tourist islands in Andaman.
- Scuba diving: Certified PADI / SSI dive centers operate in Havelock for beginner Discover Scuba Dives (DSD) and certification courses.
